# Thesis Outline

Create docs/00-project-outline.md with problem, goals, scope, users, features, out of scope, contribution, draft architecture, draft database, test plan, Docker plan, and acceptance criteria.

The default scope is a graduation MVP. Limit roles, modules, workflows, reports, and data to what can be implemented, tested, demonstrated, and defended within the project timeline. Explicitly list production-scale or advanced features as out of scope unless separately approved.

Do not choose technology before understanding the problem. Propose a stack after the outline; students may choose technology freely.

Move to WAITING_FOR_APPROVAL and stop. Only manager confirmation in chat may move the project to APPROVED.
